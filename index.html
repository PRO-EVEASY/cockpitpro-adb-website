Before you order

Frequently asked questions.

Does it work on every Chery, Jaecoo or Omoda?

No. Support depends on the head unit, device details and firmware. We check your information before accepting an order.

What information do you need?

Your vehicle model, firmware version and the engineering-menu identifier or QR information requested for your head unit. Send screenshots via WhatsApp so we can confirm the correct inputs.

Can I use the same key on another car?

Do not assume that you can. Keys are issued for the confirmed device details; date requirements and validity depend on the authorization method.

Does the key include Cockpit Pro apps or cluster navigation?

The €20 offer is for the key service. App installation, driver settings and cluster features are separate and depend on vehicle support.

When will I receive the key, and what if it doesn’t work?

We agree on delivery timing and the applicable support and refund terms before payment. If a file is not accepted, contact us with the error screen and the original device details.

Can I buy keys for customer vehicles?

Yes, enquiries from installers are welcome for vehicles they are explicitly authorized to service. Contact us for quantities and a quote.
